      Controls

      How to move and play

      DesktopClick the view to look with the mouse (or drag). W A S D or arrows walk, Shift faster. Click to use what the ring highlights. E inspect / put down. Esc frees the mouse. M sound, L research overlay, P pass, C seated height, H help.
      TouchLeft stick walks. Drag anywhere else to look. Tap a highlighted object to use it. E button = inspect / put down.
      Meta QuestLeft stick walks (head-relative), right stick snap-turns 30°. Hold right grip to aim a teleport, release to jump. Trigger uses. A/X sound, B/Y overlay, click left stick for seated height.

      Claw: click the joystick to take hold, steer with W A S D / arrows / the on-screen pad, then press START (or Space). Pressing START straight away also works.
